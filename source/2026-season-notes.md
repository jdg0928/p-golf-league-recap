# 2026 Season — Short Notes

## Season shape

- 14 Mondays played. Week 12 rained out, no makeup.
- 334 rounds, about 24 players in an average week, 3,006 holes played.
- Roughly 3,480 strokes over par across the season.
- Newton Commonwealth, par 70 (35/35), 4,992 to 5,354 yards. Front nine on even weeks, back nine on odd.
- 32 players in the league, 26 entered in the contest division.

## Two holes-in-one

- **Joey Desimone**, Week 1, No. 12, 6 p.m. tee time.
- **Dennis McNicholl**, Week 2, No. 9, 5:40 p.m. tee time.
- First aces in league play in at least a decade, and two in two weeks.
- Two of the season's 10 eagles were these. Joey Desimone's was the only eagle he made all year.

## Points race

- Scoring: 10 for the win, then 8-7-6-5-4-3-2, and a point for everyone else. USGA ties.
- **Jason Gerdom** 67 points, on the only perfect attendance in the league (14 of 14).
- **Logan Slattery** 58, **Jim Madden** 55, **Austin Isaacson** 47, **Trent Gutstein** 43, **Dan Cuddy** 41.
- Efficiency: Jim Madden scored 55 points in just 8 starts, nearly 7 per round, best in the league. Austin Isaacson better than 5 per start over 9 rounds.
- Ringer Scorecard: **Jason Gerdom** 48, which is 22 under par over the full 18. Jim Madden led it the first six weeks.

## The course, hole by hole

- Front and back play nearly the same: front +10.46 over par as a group, back +10.39.
- **Hardest**: No. 2 (par 5, 6.72 avg, one birdie all season); No. 17 (par 4, 5.66, more than half of scores double or worse); No. 5 (par 5, 6.45, shortest of the three front par 5s).
- **Most forgiving**: No. 4 (par 3, 3.84, the only hole that played under four strokes); No. 8 (par 5, 5.89, 16 birdies, most on the card); No. 6 (par 4, 4.94, 12 birdies and as many pars as bogeys).
- **Most bogeys on any hole**: No. 12 with 88. Seldom a par, seldom a disaster, almost always a bogey.
- **Most pars on any hole**: No. 13 with 71.
- No. 3 is a two-tiered green, played to the harder back pin every week, which is why a short par 3 ranked 4th-hardest.
- No. 11 played closer to a par 3½ during drainage and retaining-pond construction but was scored to its par 4, so its average is not comparable. Work is complete.

## Storylines

- **Metronome**: Logan Slattery, 62 pars, par or better on 66.7% of holes, only 2 triple-bogey-or-worse holes all season.
- **Fireworks without blow-ups**: Trent Gutstein made 3 eagles and zero triples across 108 holes. Austin Isaacson also went the whole season without a triple.
- **Great swing, no hardware**: Joey Desimone, 4th-best gross average, tied-most birdies, and a hole-in-one, but 23rd in points.
- **Heart of the league**: Brandon Bogrette led in triple-bogeys-or-worse (about 4 a round) yet still earned 34 points (T9). Bob Fitzgerald reached par or better on only 6% of holes but played 13 of 14 weeks.
